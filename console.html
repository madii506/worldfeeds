open the WORLD launchpad.

Three transactions, each built here and signed in your own wallet. The wallet that opens the launchpad is the vault: Raydium LaunchLab pays the launchpad's 1% fee from every $WORLD trade into it when you claim, and from there it goes to World Central Kitchen.

01open the launchpad

Creates the WORLD launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab: 1% of every trade on the bonding curve to this wallet, no creator fee, and at graduation half the liquidity burned and half kept as a Raydium fee key. One launchpad per wallet.

connect a wallet to see its launchpad

02launch $WORLD

Creates the coin on the launchpad: name WORLD, ticker WORLD, our globe as its image, 1 billion supply, 85 SOL to graduate to a Raydium pool. Add an opening buy if you want one; it goes through the same curve as everyone's.

03claim the kitchen's fees

Moves everything $WORLD has paid the launchpad from LaunchLab's fee vault into this wallet, ready to send to World Central Kitchen through The Giving Block.

04switch the site on

Once the coin exists, add these as environment variables on the Vercel project and redeploy. The site then shows the contract address, the buy buttons, the vault and the ledger on its own.
