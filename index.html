01 the rule

one rule. all of it goes to the kitchen.

pump.fun pays a coin's creator a cut of every trade. on $WORLD, that cut is not ours. it belongs to the kitchen, and you can watch it get there.

  1. 01.1

    every creator fee lands in one public wallet.

    The creator fee is 0.30% of every trade on the bonding curve, and between 0.05% and 0.95% after the coin graduates to PumpSwap, set by pump.fun's fee table. It is collected in SOL and claimed into the vault, a wallet that exists only for this.

  2. 01.2

    everything in the vault goes to World Central Kitchen.

    Through WCK's own crypto donation page on The Giving Block, at least once a day. No middle wallet, no team share, no "we'll donate later".

  3. 01.3

    every send is public.

    Each SOL that leaves the vault appears in plate 06 with its transaction, read straight from Solana. Nothing counts as given until it has left.

  4. 01.4

    a person presses send.

    Claiming the fees and donating them is done by hand, from the dev wallet. Here is what that person could get wrong, and where you would see it.

    05 the kitchen

    who gets the money.

    World Central Kitchen was founded in 2010 by the chef José Andrés, after the earthquake in Haiti. When a hurricane, an earthquake, a flood or a war takes a place's kitchens away, they bring cooks, and work with local chefs and restaurants to serve fresh, hot meals to the people who need them.

    We picked them because their work is the simplest thing a fee can turn into: food, for someone, today. They take crypto through The Giving Block, which is how the vault pays them.

    They have not endorsed $WORLD and are not involved in it. A donation is not an endorsement. If you'd rather give without a coin in between, you can do that right now, and you should.

    07 questions

    the obvious questions.

    is this run by World Central Kitchen?

    No. $WORLD is an independent memecoin. WCK has not endorsed it, is not involved, and owes it nothing. The coin sends money to WCK through the same public crypto donation page anyone can use.

    why not use pump.fun's built-in charity coins?

    pump.fun's Charity Coins route fees through donate.gg, and World Central Kitchen is not on donate.gg. Rather than pick a charity we didn't want, the vault claims the fees and donates them by hand, and every send is listed here so you can check it.

    how much of every trade goes to the kitchen?

    The whole creator fee, which pump.fun sets at 0.30% of every trade on the bonding curve and between 0.05% and 0.95% after graduation, depending on market cap. Work out any trade in plate 03. The rest of each trade's fee goes to pump.fun and to the pool, as on every pump.fun coin.

    what stops the dev from keeping the fees?

    Nothing on-chain does. What you get instead is a public trail: the vault's balance and every send out of it, read live from Solana, with the recipient and a receipt beside each one. If the vault fills and stops emptying, this page shows it before anyone has to say so.

    how often does the vault donate?

    At least once a day, the whole balance minus a few cents of network fees. Small balances may wait until they're worth more than the cost of sending them.

    does the team hold a supply or take a cut?

    No cut of the fees, ever: the rule is all of it. Any tokens the dev buys at launch are bought on the bonding curve like anyone else's, and will be disclosed on our 𝕏 at launch.

    what is the world map?

    Live alerts from GDACS, the disaster alert system run by the UN and the European Commission. It shows where earthquakes, floods, cyclones, wildfires and droughts are happening this week. It does not show where WCK is cooking; WCK publishes that on wck.org.

    is this financial advice?

    No. $WORLD is a memecoin. Its price can go to zero, and buying it is not a donation: only the creator fees from trading go to the kitchen. If you want to give, give directly.

    $WORLD is an independent memecoin, not affiliated with or endorsed by World Central Kitchen, The Giving Block or pump.fun. Nothing here is financial advice.
